Questions

Before You Ask

The things people want to know before sending that first message. If yours isn't here, just ask me directly — I answer my own messages.

I've never lifted before. Is this too advanced?

No. Everyone runs their own program, so a first-timer and someone with ten years under the bar can train in the same hour without either one being held back. Beginners often do best here — you learn the lifts correctly the first time instead of undoing habits later.

What's the difference between this and a group class?

A class gives everyone the same workout. Here, three people in the same room are doing three different programs. I'm coaching individually — just three at a time instead of one.

What if I have an injury?

Tell me before your first session. Most injuries mean we work around a movement, not that you can't train. If it's something that needs a medical professional first, I'll say so. [Note: Steven is a coach, not a medical provider.]

[Cancellation window and make-up policy.]

What happens when all twelve spots are full?

The roster closes and I start a waitlist. I don't add a thirteenth client — the cap is the entire point of how this works.

How long until I see results?

Strength numbers usually move within four to six weeks. Visible change takes longer and depends heavily on what you're doing the other twenty-three hours of the day. Anyone promising you a specific timeline hasn't met you yet.

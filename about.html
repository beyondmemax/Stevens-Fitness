Why Twelve

The Roster Is Capped
On Purpose

  1. I know your training history. Not from a note in an app — from having coached every session of it.
  2. Your program actually changes. Small roster means I can adjust week to week instead of running everyone through the same block.
  3. Your slot is yours. Same hour, same group, every week. No booking scramble, no showing up to a full floor.
  4. I answer my own messages. There's no front desk. You text me, I reply.

The Training

Three Clients.
One Coach.
One Hour.

Semi-private isn't a group class with a smaller room. Everyone in the session is running their own program — I'm just coaching three of them at once instead of one.

Inside a Session

How the Hour Runs

First 10 minutes

Warm-up & check-in

Movement prep tailored to what you're lifting that day, plus a quick read on how you're feeling. Sleep, soreness, stress — it all changes what we do.

Middle 35

Your main work

Your program, not the group's. I rotate between the three of you, coaching every working set that matters and loading you appropriately.

Last 15

Accessories & conditioning

Targeted accessory work for your weak points, then conditioning scaled to your level — not whatever's on the whiteboard.

What We Train

Everything Else After

Strength

Conditioning

Movement quality

Who It's For

Honest Fit Check

This works well if you want consistent coaching, you like training around other people without it being a class, and you'd rather build something over months than chase a six-week transformation.

This is probably wrong if you need a completely flexible schedule, you want one-on-one privacy, or you're prepping for a specific competition that needs daily individualized attention.

If you're not sure which one you are, ask. I'd rather tell you it's not a fit than take your money for four months.
